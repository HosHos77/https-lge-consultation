Step 4 — Piping type
Concealed wall piping or standard installation?
Ask the customer — concealed piping follows a different procedure that starts with pressure-testing the pipes.
Concealed wall piping
Pressure-test first, then the check visit, then installation
Read to the customer
“In this case we need to log a pipe pressure-test by the technician first, at EGP 450. Then 48 hours later the technician visits again to confirm there is no leak, and if everything is fine the AC is installed.”
1
First visit — pipe pressure-test at EGP 450 by an LG technician.
2
Second visit after 48 hours — to confirm no leak; once confirmed, the AC is installed.
If the customer says an outside party did the pressure-test
Read to the customer
“We apologise — the pressure-test must be done by an LG technician to confirm the piping is suitable for installing the unit correctly, so it is not affected in the future and no leak occurs.”

RAC — AC inspection
Rules for AC inspection visits
When a customer requests an inspection before buying an AC, charge as follows.
1
From one to three units, charge one visit only = EGP 300.
2
For more units, charge one visit per three units = one order at EGP 300.
Examples
Inspecting 5 units is charged as two visits = 300 + 300 EGP.
Inspecting 10 units is charged as 3 visits = 300 + 300 + 300 EGP.
Logging the inspection order
For an inspection order, log one order only regardless of the number of units, and state the number inside the order.
Example: for more than 10 units recalculate the same way — if the customer has 17 ACs, every 3 units is one order, i.e. 300 × 6 EGP.
Important — must be told to the customer
When buying from any dealer on the free-installation list: the tax invoice must be ready for the technician to review before installation.

Service Network center
Service Network centre rules — AC installation
1
The centre has priority for logging AC installation orders.
2
It appears only in Cairo and Alexandria.
3
For an AC installation order, log it dated the next day, not the same day.
4
If the customer has more than one unit, book the morning slots (first and second).
5
Never promise the customer the visit will be at the end of the slot — e.g. the fourth slot 3:30 to 5:30: you cannot promise 5:30. Leave this to the technician to coordinate with the customer.
6
For AC installation only: if the customer has more than one unit, log as many orders as units — one order is not enough.
7
Do not log any order in Madinaty or a compound after the third slot — the city does not allow technicians in after 4 PM. On official holidays, ask the customer about the compound's working hours and set the appointment accordingly.
8
The centre does not operate on official holidays, so no orders are logged on it then.

No Capacity Process
Installation orders only
When to follow this process
If a customer calls to log an installation order and we had to apologise for one of the following reasons:
No available slots on the system.
Or no available centres.
Or there is a Reclaim request and the centre is not showing on the system.
Follow these steps
1
Log the customer's full details and write inside the inquiry why the installation order could not be logged.
2
Select the Symptom that was meant to be logged on the order.
3
Assign the Call Back Agent field to Capacity Availability. The Service Network team reviews the inquiries, tries to open slots and show the centre, then transfers the inquiry from No Capacity Process to Alshaima.
4
The Schedule Complain department will call the customer within the day. For an installation order select Delivery/Installation : Installation schedule Check and Change visit time.
Script — read to the customer
Agent: “I apologise, unfortunately there are no slots available to log the request. I have raised a request to the responsible department to arrange a slot for you, and I kindly ask you to wait for them to confirm the visit date with you.”
Customer: “When will they call me?”
Agent: “Within the day, sir.”
If the customer calls back after the request to add a model or an extra number — add it on the system and update the open inquiry.
If the customer calls to cancel the order — close the inquiry and update it before closing.
If the customer contacts us after 5:30 PM or on Friday — tell him we will follow up the next day to confirm the visit date.
